About

I am a senior Mechanical Engineering student at the University of Utah graduating in May 2026, working on a senior capstone to design and build a large-scale kinetic art installation for the J.W. Marriott Jr. Institute using flip-disc technology and electromagnets.

I was born in Reno, Nevada, grew up in Newport Beach, California, and built my first computer at twelve with my brother — which sparked my curiosity about how systems come together. I graduated high school as valedictorian and a National Merit Scholar. Both brothers are engineers, so technical problem-solving has always been part of my environment.

I am looking for mechanical, systems, or robotics roles where I can take ownership of meaningful technical challenges, contribute to complex physical systems, and keep growing as an engineer.
